What can a home owner do in their portal?

This determines the complexity of the owner-facing side of the app.

A

Read-only viewer

Owners can see their unit's technical data, financial info, and download documents the company has uploaded. No uploads, no requests. Simple and safe.

B

View + upload their own documents

Owners can view everything and also upload their own files (contracts, payment receipts, personal docs). Company staff can see what the owner uploaded.

C

View + upload + submit requests/messages

Full two-way portal: owners can view data, manage documents, and also send requests or messages to the company (warranty claims, questions, change requests).
