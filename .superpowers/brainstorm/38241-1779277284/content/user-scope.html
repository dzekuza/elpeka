Who uses this CMS?

This shapes the entire auth model, data isolation, and whether we need multi-tenancy.

A

Single organisation / internal tool

One real estate company or agency. Everyone logging in belongs to the same org. Simple roles: admin, editor, viewer. No tenant isolation needed.

B

Multi-tenant SaaS

Multiple independent agencies each with their own properties, users, and data. Strict tenant isolation in the DB. More complex, but scales to selling the platform to many clients.

C

Single agency + client portal

One agency manages everything internally, but individual property owners or buyers can log in to a read-only portal to see "their" listings.
